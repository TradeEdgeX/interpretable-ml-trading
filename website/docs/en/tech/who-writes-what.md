# Who writes what

**One-liner:** Of the five template boxes, “mechanism, expected regime, falsification” are yours; “contract, landing” are translated by the AI into YAML; the “conclusion” is yours after reading the table.

## The split

| Box | Who writes it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism | You | This is your understanding of the market; the AI can’t replace it |
| Expected regime | You | You know which window this sentence wants to earn from |
| Contract | AI translates | Turning plain language into YAML is mechanical |
| Falsification | You | Writing in advance which window voids it is your judgment |
| Landing | AI translates | Picking the court and feature store is mechanical |
| Conclusion | You | Concluding after reading the table needs your understanding of strategy and regime |

## Why this split

The AI is good at translating and retelling, but not at judging “is this sentence losing in the recent window because the market changed”. That judgment needs your understanding of the strategy and the regime, so “mechanism, expected regime, falsification, conclusion” are yours; “contract, landing” are translated by the AI.
