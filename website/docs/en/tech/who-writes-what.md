# Who writes what

**One line:** Humans write the sentence and the verdict; the program writes numbers; the AI fills the template, orchestrates commands, and retells KPIs — no topic change, no declaring for you.

## Division

| Who | Writes | Does not write |
|---|---|---|
| **Human** | The sentence, `verdict` | Letting the AI declare |
| **Program** | Feature store, three-window backtest, `gate_status`, five KPIs | `verdict` |
| **AI** | Template cleanup, structural checks, command orchestration, report retell | Web as evidence; hand-editing `verdict` |

Without “measure this”: only validate, lineage lookup, and init.
